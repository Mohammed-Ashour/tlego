      Data sources

      Your copy of Khonsu fetches this data, keeps a local copy to limit requests and credits each source. Khonsu doesn't redistribute it. Space weather and launch times are requested when the app opens and send nothing about you or your satellites. The Identity and Radio tabs send the selected satellite's NORAD ID to Wikidata and SatNOGS, and to Space-Track if you've connected an account. For Sentinel-2 and Landsat satellites, the Sees tab sends the platform name to Earth Search. Your location is only sent if you ask for cloud cover, and is rounded to about 10 km first.
